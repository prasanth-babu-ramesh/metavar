The Core Product: BioReadiness Screen™

A predictive go/no-go decision layer implemented before formal certification testing.

Bioaccessible?

Has weathering, oxidation, or pretreatment made the plastic-derived carbon physically and chemically reachable to biology?

Assimilable?

Are microbes utilizing the polymer backbone, or just consuming additives and leachates? Verified via CCR-informed biological logic.

Ready?

Does the evidence mandate advancing to certified testing labs, or hold back for targeted formulation adjustment?
